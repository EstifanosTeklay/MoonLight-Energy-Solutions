# Solar Potential Analysis for Strategic Investment

MoonLight Energy Solutions – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

📌 Business Objective

MoonLight Energy Solutions aims to enhance operational efficiency and long-term sustainability through targeted solar energy investments. This analysis evaluates environmental and solar radiation measurements across three West African countries — Benin, Sierra Leone, and Togo  to identify high-potential regions for solar installation.

The goal of this study is to:

•  Extract key trends from solar and environmental data

•  Translate statistical findings into actionable strategy

•  Support data-driven decision-making for sustainable expansion

📊 Dataset Overview

The analysis compares annual aggregated solar and environmental metrics across the three countries, focusing on:

•  Annual Global Horizontal Irradiance (GHI)

•  Average Peak Sun Hours (PSH)

•  Diffuse Fraction & Beam Ratio

•  Solar Variability (Hourly & Daily GHI Standard Deviation)

•  Climate Conditions (Temperature, Wind, Precipitation)

These indicators collectively determine solar energy yield, system efficiency, and operational risk.

 Key Insights on Solar Resource Availability

•  Sierra Leone records the highest Annual GHI and Peak Sun Hours, indicating strong solar potential.

•  Benin follows closely with competitive solar exposure and balanced radiation characteristics.

•  Togo shows noticeably lower GHI and PSH, limiting its overall energy generation capacity.

📌 Insight: High solar irradiance alone does not guarantee feasibility—stability and climate factors matter.

🌤️ Atmospheric Conditions & Solar Quality

•  Togo has the highest Diffuse Fraction, meaning a larger share of sunlight is scattered by clouds and moisture.

•  Benin and Sierra Leone maintain higher Beam Ratios, favoring direct solar radiation—ideal for photovoltaic efficiency.

📌 Insight: Regions with higher direct beam radiation provide better panel performance and predictability.

📉 Solar Variability & Reliability

•  Sierra Leone exhibits low hourly variability but high daily fluctuations, increasing grid integration and storage challenges.

•  Togo shows the highest variability overall, increasing operational uncertainty.

•  Benin demonstrates moderate and stable variability, supporting consistent power generation.

📌 Insight: Lower variability translates to lower system stress, reduced storage costs, and higher reliability.

🌡️ Climate & Environmental Risk

•  Sierra Leone experiences very high annual precipitation, which may affect panel maintenance, degradation, and downtime.

•  Togo has extreme rainfall levels, posing flooding and infrastructure risks.

•  Benin maintains moderate precipitation, manageable temperatures, and favorable wind conditions.

📌 Insight: Climate stability is critical for long-term asset protection and operational efficiency.

🎯 Strategic Recommendation
✅ Primary Investment Target: Benin

•  Benin emerges as the optimal location for solar installations, offering:

•  Strong and stable solar irradiance

•  High direct beam radiation

•  Lower variability and climate risk

•  Favorable conditions for scalable, long-term projects

•  This aligns strongly with MoonLight Energy Solutions sustainability and efficiency goals.

⚠️ Secondary Opportunity: Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone presents high solar yield potential but requires:

•  Enhanced system design (storage & drainage)

•  Higher maintenance planning due to rainfall

•  Risk-adjusted investment strategy

•  Recommended for selective or pilot-scale deployment.

🚫 Not Recommended (Short-Term): Togo

•  High atmospheric diffusion, extreme precipitation, and variability make Togo less suitable for immediate solar investment. Consider reassessment only after further localized or seasonal studies.
